FGV Arts, my personal fantasy illustration project.

Hi everyone! I'm from Chile. I create original creatures, spirits and fantasy worlds inspired by mythology, nature and imagination. Every illustration is part of a growing universe where each character has its own identity and story.

My current main collection is Spirits of the Universe, a series of elemental beings representing the forces of nature.
